04 / Poke the interrogation policy

Allocate the same 196 paired measurements among 98 cells. Uniform repeats each cell twice. Adaptive observes all once, then asks about the least confident remaining cells. No labels are used during allocation.

The active policy is hand-designed, not learned. Gains are conditional on the assumed shot-noise distribution.

06 / What this did not prove

Loop holonomy is a known invariant. A graph that stores two binary images in 224 bytes does not beat a 13-byte bitset. Scrambling the gauge is a restricted, mathematically designed change of coordinates—not an arbitrary physical perturbation.

The useful test is narrower: does relational encoding support stable updates and useful interrogation when local edge values themselves cannot be trusted as absolute coordinates?

Φ□ = φtop + φright − φbottom − φleft (mod 2π) z₁ = e^(i(φtop+φright)) z₂ = e^(i(φleft+φbottom)) z₁ · conj(z₂) = e^(iΦ□)

Under a vertex coordinate rephasing α, every oriented edge φᵤᵥ becomes φᵤᵥ + αᵥ − αᵤ. Every closed loop retains its phase.

To explore beyond this mathematical control, the next gate needs an experience-trained writer and interrogation policy that beats conventional memory on unforeseen structured questions with matched storage and compute.
